# RideShareEU — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** a map pin laid on its side becomes the car. Its point is the nose, its hole is the rear window, and two
  wheels sit below. Your pickup point comes to you.
- **Versions:** horizontal (primary) · stacked · symbol only · app icon (white car on a maroon rounded square).
- **Files:**
  - Screens: `export/horizontal/`, `export/stacked/`, `export/symbol/` (SVG and PNG).
  - Web and app icons: `export/symbol/` (`favicon.ico`, `favicon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192/512.png`,
    `maskable-512.png`, `site.webmanifest`).
  - Masters: `symbol.svg`, `symbol-small.svg`, `lockup-horizontal.svg`, `lockup-stacked.svg`. They are generated by
    `build_wordmark.py` and then `build_logo.py`; change the scripts, not the SVGs.
- **In the app:** `src/components/Logo.tsx` (the car on its maroon chip), `src/components/Wordmark.tsx` (the name),
  `src/app/icon.svg` (favicon), `src/app/apple-icon.png` (iPhone home screen).

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone around the logo equal to **one wheel's diameter** on every side. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 16 mm wide |
| Symbol | 24 px (16–24 px: use `symbol-small.svg`, the cut with a bigger window and wheels) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enverga Maroon | #800000 | 128 0 0 | 0 100 100 50 | Match against a swatch book; candidates: 7624 C, 188 C |
| Maroon (dark mode) | #C2455A | 194 69 90 | 0 64 54 24 | Screens only |
| Ink | #111827 | 17 24 39 | 56 38 0 85 | — |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | — |

White on Enverga Maroon has a contrast ratio of about 10.9:1.

**Approved pairs:** maroon on white · white on maroon · ink on white · white on ink.
On photos, use the white version on a calm area, or the app icon (car on the maroon tile).

## 5. Typography
- **Logo name:** custom monoline lettering (`Wordmark.tsx`). It isn't a font, so never retype the name to make a
  logo; use the files.
- **App interface:** the app's existing system font stack (Inter where installed, then the device font).

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch, squash or rotate the car · don't flip it (it always drives to the right) · don't recolour outside the
palette · don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects · don't fill in the window · don't move the wheels or change
the gap above them · don't resize the parts of a lockup separately · don't type the name in another font next to the
car.

## 7. Before public launch
- Run a trademark search (IPOPHL, plus a reverse image search). This design hasn't been checked for clearance.
- Print a car-window sticker and a lanyard badge to check the mark at real size.

Master files: `docs/brand/logo/`. Concept history: `docs/brand/logo-concepts/`.
